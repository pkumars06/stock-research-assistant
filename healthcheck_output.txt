   Rule: Looks fine when one-off items are under 10.0% of profit, tax is between 15.0% and 35.0% of profit (the usual company rate in India is about 25%), and other income is under 30.0% of profit before tax.
   Finding: Profit before tax was ₹202.89 crore and profit after tax ₹149.47 crore in FY26. There were no one-off (exceptional) items. Tax took 26.3% of profit before tax.    What it means: Profit can be lifted by things that will not happen again: a one-off gain, an unusually low tax bill, or income from investments rather than from selling the company's products.
